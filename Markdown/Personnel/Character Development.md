# Creation Steps
- Determine Background ([p. 43](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=43))
- Determine Duty ([p. 46](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=46))
- Select Species. Determines starting XP/Traits. ([p. 51](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=51))
- Select Career ([p. 61](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=61)) and assign 1 rank in 4 career skills for free.
- Select Specialization ([pp. 61-101](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=61)) and assign 1 rank in 2 specialization skills for free.
- Invest XP on traits, skills, talents, specialization, and duty value.
- Determine derived attributes: wound, strain, defense, and soak
- Determine Motiviation ([p. 104](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=104))
- Choose Gear and Appearance. 500 credits are provided from the Rebellion. Roll d100 to represent personal funds for use after creation. ([pp. 108, 161](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=108))
- Acquire Rebellion Resources. Communicate with GM on player needs. ([p. 111](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=111))
- Unspent experience from creation carries over in-game.

# XP Investing
- Can increase traits only during character creation, up to rank 5. Costs 10x rank. ([p. 102](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#p=102)) Does not exceed 6 during play.
- Can increase skill rank, up to rank 2 in creation. Costs 5x rank, +5 for out of career skill.
- Can purchase talents within specialization at it's specificied cost.
- Can purchase new specializations, costs 10x number of owned specializations, including new specialization (+10 for outside career specializations).
