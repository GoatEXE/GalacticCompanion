# Crew and Large Craft
- Vehicle encounters use the normal combat framework, but the GM should focus on the crew actions that matter to the scene. ([p. 244](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=245))
- On silhouette 5+ craft, movement and facing may be handled more abstractly while gunners, engineers, commanders, and slicers resolve their own stations. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))
- If a character has no obvious station, they can assist, repair damage, scan, coordinate fire, repel boarders, or solve a scene-specific problem. ([p. 250](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=251))

# Chases
- Chases use competitive Piloting checks, starting from a chosen range band. ([p. 256](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))
- The winner may open or close the gap, with speed differences shaping how much the range changes. ([p. 256](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))
- A chase ends when the pursuer reaches close or engaged range, or when the target escapes beyond extreme range. ([p. 256](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))
- Chases can run as their own encounter or happen inside a larger fight while other crew members keep acting. ([p. 256](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))

# Terrain and Navigation
- Terrain, obstacles, debris, crowded traffic, and stellar hazards can reduce speed, increase Piloting difficulty, or require checks for maneuvers that would otherwise be automatic. ([p. 255](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=256))
- Failed terrain checks can cost speed or prevent the intended movement. Walkers use terrain rules suited to ground movement. ([p. 255](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=256), [p. 266](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=267))
- Astrogation handles hyperspace routes. Bad charts, damaged navigation systems, ship damage, rushed jumps, gravity wells, and mass shadows can make the jump harder or impossible. ([pp. 261-262](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=262))

# Groups and Squadrons
- Identical minion vehicles and crews can be grouped to keep large dogfights manageable, similar to minion groups. ([p. 253](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=254))
- Treat the group as one operating unit for checks and damage resolution instead of tracking a separate damage pool for each member. ([p. 253](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=254))
- The vehicle remains the thing suffering damage; use groups when individual tracking would slow the scene without adding interesting decisions. ([p. 253](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=254))
