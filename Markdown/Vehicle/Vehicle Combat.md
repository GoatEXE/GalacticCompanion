# Encounter Structure
- Vehicle encounters use the normal initiative and turn structure, with vehicle-specific maneuvers and actions layered on top. ([p. 244](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=245))
- Each crew member still acts on a PC or NPC slot as usual, but the vehicle is limited in how many pilot-only maneuvers and actions it can benefit from each round. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))
- Small craft depend heavily on the pilot's choices. Large ships usually treat the pilot's movement as only one part of a broader crew encounter. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))

# Vehicle Turns
- Silhouette 1-4 vehicles may perform one free pilot-only maneuver each round, and may take a second by suffering 2 system strain. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))
- Silhouette 5+ vehicles may perform only one pilot-only maneuver each round. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))
- A vehicle may benefit from only one pilot-only action each round, no matter how many pilots or crew members are aboard. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))
- Speed is the vehicle's current pace within its maximum, not a requirement to move every round. Use maneuvers and range bands to describe the actual position change. ([p. 246](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=247))

# Attacks
- Vehicle weapons usually use <b>Gunnery</b>. Some mounted or personal-scale weapons may use another combat skill if the weapon profile calls for it. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- Vehicle and starship attack difficulty is based on the firing vessel's silhouette compared to the target's silhouette, not the personal-scale range difficulty table. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- Same silhouette, or firing vessel within 1 silhouette of the target, is Average difficulty. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- If the firing vessel is 2 or more silhouettes smaller than the target, the attack is Easy. If it is larger than the target, difficulty scales by difference: 2 larger is Hard, 3 larger is Daunting, and 4 or more larger is Formidable. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- Planetary-scale range bands such as Close, Short, Medium, Long, and Extreme still govern positioning, movement, and whether a target is in reach. ([pp. 253-255](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=254))
- Apply defense from the targeted zone as setback dice, and respect weapon firing arcs when choosing a target. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))

# Damage and Critical Hits
- A successful vehicle attack adds +1 damage for each uncancelled success, then armor reduces the total. Remaining damage becomes hull trauma. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- Most vehicle weapons use planetary scale. When crossing scales, each point of vehicle weapon damage, armor, or hull trauma equals 10 points of equivalent personal-scale damage, soak, or wounds. ([p. 236](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=237))
- A vehicle can suffer a Critical Hit when an attack deals damage after armor and the attacker activates the weapon's critical rating. Existing Critical Hits add +10 to later vehicle critical rolls. ([p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))
- Common advantage or triumph spends include triggering weapon qualities, inflicting a Critical Hit, improving an ally's next check, or creating a positional opening. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
