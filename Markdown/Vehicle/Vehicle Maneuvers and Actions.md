# Pilot Maneuvers
- <b>Accelerate/Decelerate</b>: Increase or decrease current speed by 1, within the vehicle's speed limits. ([p. 246](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=247))
- <b>Fly/Drive</b>: Move the vehicle relative to terrain, objects, or other vehicles. Current speed controls how far the vehicle can shift by range band. ([p. 246](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=247))
- <b>Evasive Maneuvers</b>: Make the vehicle harder to hit, but also make its own attacks less accurate. Usually limited to faster, smaller craft. ([p. 246](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=247))
- <b>Stay on Target</b>: Improve the vehicle's attacks, but make the vehicle easier to hit in return. Usually limited to faster, smaller craft. ([p. 246](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=247))
- <b>Punch It</b>: Jump immediately to maximum speed and suffer system strain for the sudden stress. ([p. 247](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=248))
- <b>Angle Deflector Shields</b>: Move 1 point of defense from one defense zone to another. ([p. 247](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=248))

# Pilot Actions
- <b>Gain the Advantage</b>: Make a Piloting check to out-position a target. Success can cancel the target's evasive maneuvers against the attacker and lets the attacker choose which defense zone applies. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- <b>Vehicle Attack</b>: Fire a vehicle weapon with the appropriate combat skill, usually Gunnery. Apply range, silhouette, defense, firing arc, and other modifiers. ([p. 248](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- Only one pilot-only action can affect the same vehicle each round, even if more than one character could attempt it. ([p. 245](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=246))

# Crew Support Actions
- <b>Co-Pilot</b>: Use Piloting to help the pilot; each success downgrades the pilot's next Piloting check by 1. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- <b>Plot Course</b>: Use Astrogation or a related check to reduce terrain, navigation, or path-related setbacks for the pilot. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- <b>Scan the Enemy</b>: Use Perception to learn a target's weapons, modifications, and damage state; extra success can reveal hull trauma and system strain. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- <b>Fire Discipline</b>: Use Leadership or Discipline to coordinate shipboard fire and aid allied attacks; extra results can extend benefits or add tactical pressure per the action entry. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))

# Engineering and Electronic Warfare
- <b>Damage Control</b>: Use Mechanics to recover 1 system strain per success. If the GM allows combat hull repair, it is limited to once per encounter and repairs 1 hull trauma per success. ([pp. 248-249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- <b>Boost Shields</b>: Use Mechanics to briefly raise one defense zone, at the cost of system strain. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- <b>Slice Enemy Systems</b>: Use Computers to interfere with enemy defenses or systems for a short time; extra results can worsen the disruption per the action entry. ([p. 249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=250))
- Other crew can still take normal actions, assist checks, manage gear, or use vehicle stations when the scene supports it. ([p. 250](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=251))
