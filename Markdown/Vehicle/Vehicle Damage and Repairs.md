# Thresholds and Strain
- <b>Hull Trauma Threshold</b> measures structural damage. Damage that remains after armor is applied becomes hull trauma. ([p. 237](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=238))
- <b>System Strain Threshold</b> measures overload, power stress, and failing systems. Maneuvers, enemy effects, and hazards can all inflict system strain. ([p. 238](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=239))
- When hull trauma exceeds threshold, the vehicle is crippled. Small or insignificant craft may explode or be disabled at the GM's discretion; larger craft typically suffer a Critical Hit and are disabled or crippled until repaired or restored. ([pp. 236-238](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=237), [p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))
- When system strain exceeds threshold, systems overload and shut down. Speed, defense, and available systems can be severely reduced; consult the vehicle damage rules for the exact result. ([pp. 236-238](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=237), [p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))

# Critical Hits
- A vehicle suffers a Critical Hit when an attack deals damage past armor and the attacker activates the weapon's critical rating, or when the rules call for one because of severe damage. ([p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))
- Roll on the vehicle Critical Hit table. Each existing vehicle Critical Hit adds +10 to future rolls against that vehicle. ([p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))
- Critical Hits can damage components, reduce handling or speed, knock out shields or weapons, damage engines, or destroy the vehicle outright at the worst results. ([pp. 257-259](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))
- Repairing a Critical Hit requires a Mechanics check at the difficulty listed for that result. ([p. 257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=258))

# Damage Control
- Damage Control is a Mechanics action used during an encounter to recover system strain; each success removes 1 system strain. ([pp. 248-249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- If the GM allows combat hull repair, Damage Control can be attempted once per encounter and repairs 1 hull trauma per success. ([pp. 248-249](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=249))
- Outside combat, system strain can recover after a full day without suffering more system strain. Hull repairs usually require time, parts, facilities, and credits. ([p. 238](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=239))

# Collisions and Hazards
- Dangerous terrain, obstacles, debris, stellar phenomena, or rushed navigation can force Piloting checks tied to speed, silhouette, and the hazard. ([p. 254](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=255))
- Minor and major collisions inflict one Critical Hit. Defense in the impacted zone reduces the critical roll by 10 per point for minor collisions or 5 per point for major collisions. ([pp. 256-257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))
- If defense reduces the collision critical roll to 0 or less, the vehicle avoids the collision's Critical Hit. ([pp. 256-257](https://online.anyflip.com/ziisf/jobq/mobile/index.html#page=257))
